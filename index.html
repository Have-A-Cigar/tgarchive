tgarchive

tgarchive is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

It keeps an archive of the owner's study chats and prepares task cards for the owner's study calendar. When a task comes with attached files, tgarchive uploads them to a folder on the owner's own Google Drive and turns on view-by-link access, so the links can be added to the task card.
