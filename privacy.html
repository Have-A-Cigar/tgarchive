Privacy policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026. Applies to tgarchive.

Who uses tgarchive

tgarchive is used only by its owner. Nobody else signs in to it, and it collects no data about other people's Google accounts.

Google user data it accesses

With the owner's consent, tgarchive uses the Google Drive API on the owner's own Google Drive. It uses this access only to:

  • create folders inside one storage folder chosen by the owner;
  • upload files of study tasks into those folders;
  • turn on "anyone with the link can view" for the files it uploads.

It does not delete, move or change other files on the Drive.

Storage and sharing

Access keys are stored only on the owner's own computer and server. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. Uploaded files stay on the owner's Google Drive, under the owner's control.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke tgarchive's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
